# Operating System

I have made my own distro that I'm using, the idea behind it is to use a minimal no bloat version of windows. Doesn't matter which one you use but make sure it's ZERO bloat version.

Looking around what I found cool were

1. xOS 11 23H2
2. KernelOS 11 23H2
3. FSOS 11 24H2

Remember these are specifically for Ryzen 7 9800X3D and Radeon RX 7900 XT

[Look up FrameSync Labs video about it!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uHjGV1NrO_Y)

If you aren't nerded outta your mind and wanna do things yourself all the way through I'd HIGHLY recommend going down the FSOS path. FrameSync has a sick discord with great resource and content for you to consume and get yourself setup. This person is also an active maintainer of the OS with new windows security updates FSOS also receives updates and a lot of troubleshooting resources on their discord was very useful for what I was doing myself.

## Notes

As much as you'd think OS matter, it does to about 1-2% it's probably not your bottleneck, unless it is. What I've noticed with my own testing of games like POE2, Valorant, Marvel Rivals, Borderlands 4 are as follows:

- Average FPS: 2.3-2.5% gain
- 1% Lows: 1.0-1.3% gain
- 0.1% Lows: 0.7-0.9% loss

Testing on same hardware as specified in pc-spec with no customization done except OS update.
